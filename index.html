About me

From audit files to automated dashboards

I'm Dolly Lokhandwala, a data analyst and Certified Fraud Examiner. I began my career in audit and tax in Mumbai, testing internal controls and tracing fraud in bank loans, cash handling and contracts. That work taught me that numbers always tell a story if you know where to look.

Today I work in public-sector technology, building Power BI reporting, Power Platform and UiPath automations, and data models for finance, timekeeping, legal and compliance teams. I enjoy the full cycle: gathering requirements, preparing the data, building the solution, and supporting the people who rely on it.
